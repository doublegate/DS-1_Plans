#### Overall Impression

The console nails the deadpan "Imperial PDR Dossier" atmosphere and turns absurdly large exponents ($10^{18}\text{ kg}$, $10^{32}\text{ J}$) into compelling engineering visual storytelling. However, it currently behaves like a static slide deck rather than an interconnected engineering workbench: the standalone HTML distributable is broken by an unpinned CDN script, the print stylesheet fails to match `Panel` and `Pill` DOM nodes, `THEME.textDim` (`#5a5f6a`) is unreadable at 2.6:1 contrast, and none of the `HW-1..10` / `DR-01..16` / `§` traceability badges are clickable.
